# 1\. Scope and who we are

This Privacy Policy ("Policy") applies to the InternSharks.ai™ website, mobile applications, APIs, communications and related services (together, the "Platform") operated by DoubleTap Innovations and Technologies Pvt Ltd. ("InternSharks", "we", "us" or "our"). It applies to students, parents or lawful guardians, recruiters, employers, educational institutions, coordinators, mentors and visitors.

For applicable Indian data-protection law, we act as the Data Fiduciary for personal data for which we determine the purpose and means of processing. Where an institution or recruiter independently decides how to use data received through the Platform, that organisation may also have its own legal obligations.

# 2\. Important definitions

**Personal data** — data about an identifiable individual, including profile, education, identity, application, communication and device data.

**AI Services** — features that use artificial intelligence or automated processing, including resume enhancement and analysis, ATS scoring, matching, career assistance, syllabus and assessment generation, KYC support, mock interviews, chatbots and profile-content creation.

**Service Provider** — an organisation that processes data for us or supports the Platform—such as cloud hosting, storage, payment, AI, messaging, analytics, security or communications providers. "Vendor" in this Policy has this service-provider meaning; it does not mean a product inventory supplier.

**Minor** — a person who has not reached the age at which they may independently consent or contract under applicable law.

# 6\. Notice, consent and choices

AI Services process the information you provide together with relevant account, profile, internship or application data. Outputs may include resume suggestions, ATS or matching scores, candidate rankings, eligibility checks, generated descriptions, interview questions, transcripts, summaries, recommendations and progress insights.

• AI outputs are decision-support tools and may be incomplete, inaccurate or biased. They are not a guarantee of selection, employability, legal compliance or performance.

• Recruiters and institutions must apply meaningful human review before making consequential admission, selection, rejection, discipline or employment decisions.

• Users may request correction of inaccurate source data and may contact us to question or seek review of a significant automated score or recommendation.

• Do not submit unnecessary sensitive or confidential information in a prompt, resume, document, image or audio recording.

• We do not intentionally use personal data submitted to the Platform to train publicly available or general-purpose AI models unless we separately explain that use and obtain any consent required by law.

Our active implementation may use OpenAI APIs and may use other AI providers configured for a particular feature. AI providers may temporarily retain inputs and outputs for security, abuse prevention or service operation under their applicable contracts and settings. Provider, model and retention configurations may change; we apply reasonable due diligence and data-minimisation controls and will update this Policy when a material change affects users.

# 9\. Recruiter and institution access controls

A valid licence, plan or credit balance is a commercial condition for certain functionality; it does not provide unrestricted access to all personal data. Recruiters and institutions receive role- and purpose-based access to information relevant to authorised recruitment, internship administration or institution management. Visibility may also depend on student settings, application status, eligibility, verification and applicable law.

• Recruiters may use candidate information only for genuine opportunities represented on the Platform and must not scrape, sell, publish, profile for unrelated purposes, or contact candidates unlawfully.

• Institutions and coordinators may access only students and records they are authorised to manage and must keep credentials confidential.

• Access may be suspended when credits or licences expire, an account is inactive, consent is withdrawn, or misuse is suspected. We may retain limited audit records after access ends.

• Recipients must delete or securely retain downloaded data according to applicable law and their own disclosed retention obligations.

# 10\. Children and users under 18

School students and other users under 18 may register and use age-appropriate features to gain early exposure to careers and internships. Before processing a Minor's personal data or enabling a Minor to accept binding terms, we require verifiable consent and agreement from a parent or lawful guardian to the extent required by law. An institution's approval is not a substitute for guardian consent unless applicable law expressly permits it.

• The parent or guardian must supervise the Minor's account and confirm that submitted information is accurate and appropriate.

• Recruiters must verify age and obtain all consents or permissions required for an internship. Any work must be lawful, age-appropriate, non-hazardous, safe and compatible with the student's education, working-hour restrictions and applicable child or adolescent labour laws.

• Registration does not mean every Minor may legally perform every internship. We may restrict, review or remove an opportunity or placement based on age, safety, law or guardian concerns.

• We do not knowingly undertake tracking, behavioural monitoring or targeted advertising directed at Minors in a manner prohibited by law.

• A parent or guardian may review, correct or request deletion of a Minor's data by contacting <grievance@internsharks.ai>, subject to identity and authority verification.

A user may initiate deletion through the mobile app's "Delete Account" function where available, or by emailing us from the registered address.
